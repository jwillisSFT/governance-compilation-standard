# Research and Validation Status

v0.1.0 is a formally structured research specification, not a completed proof or production standard.

## Present evidence

- literature-grounded architecture and semantics;
- explicit assumptions and proof obligations;
- worked cross-domain examples;
- counterexamples and negative fixtures;
- schema validation;
- finite repository semantic checks;
- bounded analytical suite with injected defects.

## Evidence still needed

- independent implementations;
- machine-checked formalization of selected properties;
- compiler/projection refinement evidence;
- interoperability testing;
- independent conformity assessment;
- deployment route/non-bypassability studies;
- controlled comparative studies;
- multi-domain operational pilots;
- longitudinal maintenance/change evidence.

The strength of any public claim should remain bounded by the strongest evidence actually obtained.
