# Open Research Problems

The specification is intentionally research-generating. The following questions are not represented as solved by v0.1.0.

## Formal semantics

1. Under which typed composition algebras can no-silent-weakening be proved compositionally?
2. What is the minimum formal structure required to establish region coverage for parametric or open-world action spaces?
3. How should defeasible/exception semantics compose with authority provenance without creating hidden precedence authority?
4. Can closure-projection soundness be mechanically proved for target languages with weaker native semantics?
5. What proof obligations are sufficient to prevent vacuous safety claims in governance profiles?

## Compiler and projection assurance

6. What refinement relation best captures semantic equivalence between GIR and Rego, Cedar, XACML, DSL, or custom target representations?
7. Which compiler qualification evidence gives the best cost/assurance tradeoff at different consequence classes?
8. Can independently implemented compilers produce semantically equivalent outputs from the same GIR/profile?

## Evidence and closure

9. How should probabilistic evidence be incorporated into deterministic governance closure without falsely converting confidence into authority?
10. What evidence-source qualification properties are necessary across cybersecurity, finance, healthcare, identity, and physical-control domains?
11. What is the minimum receipt/evidence history needed for reproducible closure without creating excessive privacy/data-retention risk?

## Institutions and computational law

12. How should a compiled package represent an authorized but substantively contested source?
13. Which ambiguities can be legitimately resolved during compilation and which must remain institutional/runtime judgment points?
14. How should successful challenges propagate through compiled packages and prior consequential decisions?
15. Can authority provenance be represented in a cross-jurisdictional interoperable form without oversimplifying institutional authority?

## Runtime/deployment assurance

16. What evidence is sufficient to establish non-bypassability for a defined deployment boundary?
17. How can route completeness be tested when privileged interfaces, emergency paths, or infrastructure APIs change dynamically?
18. What atomic/fencing patterns are sufficient substitutes for explicit commitment-boundary re-evaluation?

## Distributed transitions

19. How should CTCs compose across sagas, partial commits, irreversible external effects, and compensation?
20. What constitutes composite evidentiary closure when different sub-transitions terminate with different outcomes?
21. Who possesses recovery authority after partial commitment, and how should that authority be compiled?

## Empirical evaluation

22. Does a structured Governance Compilation workflow reduce policy-to-execution defects compared with baseline policy-as-code practice?
23. How consistent are independent modelers when deriving regions and CTCs from the same governance source?
24. What new failure modes or institutional costs does Governance Compilation introduce?
25. Does explicit authority provenance improve assessor agreement or incident reconstruction?
26. What is the adoption/maintenance cost of lifecycle evidence in real organizations?
